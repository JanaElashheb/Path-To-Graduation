DIRECTIONAL ANGLES:
In 2 dimensions, the direction of the vector is given by a single angle, but in 3-dimensions, the direction of the vectors is given by 3 angles, called DIRECTION ANGLES also known as DIRECTIONAL COSINES

The direction angles of vector [a,b,c] are (alpha),(beta),(gamma), wich are the angles the vector makes with the positive x-,y-,z-, axes

PLANES IN R(ss3):
(lesson 4 - pic 6.png)
Planes can be labelled with equations, every point on the xy-plane has a z-component of 0, that means that every coordinate has a form of (x,y,0), therefore the xy-plane is represented by the equation z = 0
That means that for the yz-plane the equation would be x = 0 and for the xz-plane the equation would be y = 0

Example:
Determine the equation of the plane containing all of the three points: A(3,2,-2), B(-1,2,2), C(-3,2,-1)
Since all the points have a y-coordinate of 2, that means that the equation of the plan would be y = 2
